# PC Build & Troubleshooting Guide

This project documents my 2024 gaming PC build and troubleshooting process with my cousin, showcasing hands-on hardware experience, problem-solving skills, and IT knowledge relevant to IT Support roles.

---

## PC Build Overview

- **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 9 7800X  
- **Motherboard:** ASUS TUF  
- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070  
- **RAM:** 32GB DDR5 6000MHz  
- **Storage:** 1TB NVMe SSD + 2TB HDD  
- **Power Supply:** Corsair RM850x 850W 80+ Gold  
- **Case:** NZXT H510  
- **Cooling:** Noctua NH-D15 Air Cooler  

---

## Upgrade & Installation Process

1. Opened the case and followed anti-static precautions.  
2. Installed the CPU, RAM, and motherboard carefully.  
3. Fitted the GPU and ensured proper power connections.  
4. Installed storage drives and connected power/data cables.  
5. Connected the PSU and tested the system before closing the case.  
6. Installed drivers and tested system stability under load.  

---

## Troubleshooting Example: Faulty Power Supply

**Problem:** The PC failed to power on after installing the GPU.  

**Actions Taken:**  
- Checked all power connections and seating of components.  
- Tested RAM and GPU individually to isolate the issue.  
- Researched online and reviewed troubleshooting guides.  
- Identified the PSU as faulty and replaced it with a stable 850W unit.  

**Result:** The system booted successfully and ran smoothly under load.  

**Importance:** This demonstrated the value of a systematic, step-by-step approach to troubleshooting and reinforced my problem-solving skills.
